Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Beyond The Vale LLC ("Beyond The Vale", "I", "me") is a software consultancy run by Cody Peterson in Boise, Idaho. This policy explains what information this website collects, and how the Max assistant application uses the Google account data you grant it.

This website

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It sets cookies and records standard details such as pages viewed, approximate location, browser, and referring site. The contact form sends the name, email, and message you type to Formspree, which forwards them to me so I can reply. I do not sell this information or share it with anyone beyond those services.

The Max assistant and your Google account

Max is a personal assistant application that Beyond The Vale operates for its own use. When you sign in with Google, Max asks for two read-only permissions:

  • Gmail, read only (gmail.readonly) — to show and summarize your mail.
  • Google Calendar, read only (calendar.readonly) — to show your events and remind you of them.

These permissions are read only. Max cannot send, label, delete, or change anything in your Gmail or Calendar.

How that data is handled

Max runs on your own computer and processes your mail and calendar there. Message subjects and senders from the last 30 days are kept in a local file so the assistant does not have to ask Google again each time; message bodies and calendar events are fetched when needed and are not stored. The access token that lets Max read your account is kept in your operating system's credential store, not in a file. None of this data is sent to Beyond The Vale or to any third party, and none of it is sold.

You can revoke Max's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited use

Max's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
